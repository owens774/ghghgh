PLAYBOOK U — PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Playbook U ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use playbookyou.com (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
(a) Account information — your first name, last name, and email address when you create an account.
(b) Payment information — when you subscribe, payments are handled by Stripe. We receive limited information such as your subscription status, customer ID, and the brand and last four digits of your card. We do NOT receive or store your full card number.
(c) Content you create — plays, notes, lineups, game plans, and settings you save in the Service.
(d) Usage and device data — basic technical information such as browser/device type and actions taken within the app, used to operate, secure, and improve the Service.
WE DO NOT SELL YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION
We use information to: create and manage your account; provide, maintain, and improve the Service; process subscriptions and prevent fraud and abuse (including enforcing one free trial per email); sync your saved content across your devices; communicate with you about your account or support requests; and comply with legal obligations.

3. HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION
We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Service, including: Stripe (payment processing), Supabase (authentication and data storage), Vercel (hosting), and, if you use them, email/SMS providers for notifications you request (such as sharing a play). These providers process information on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of us or our users.

4. COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE
The Service uses cookies and browser local storage to keep you signed in and to store certain settings and content on your device. You can clear these in your browser settings, but doing so may sign you out or reset locally stored data.

5. DATA RETENTION
We retain your account information and content while your account is active. If you delete your account or request deletion, we will delete or anonymize your information, except where we are required to retain certain records (for example, billing and tax records) or to enforce our one-free-trial-per-email rule.

6. SECURITY
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, or to opt out of certain processing. To make a request, email vinceowens44@gmail.com. We will respond as required by applicable law, including, where applicable, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. SHARED PLAYS
When you share a play by link, text message, or email, the play's contents are encoded into the shareable link so the recipient can view it. Please do not include sensitive personal information in plays you choose to share.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
We may update this Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide additional notice where appropriate.
